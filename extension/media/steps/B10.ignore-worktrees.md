# Task worktrees are git-ignored

`B10.ignore-worktrees`

Task worktrees live in .claude/worktrees/ inside the repo (D-022). Ignoring it keeps them out of your main checkout's changes and out of VSCode search.

## How to fix it

**Do it** runs the exact command the check works out for this repo, in a terminal you can see.

---

The step ticks itself when its check passes. **Check** re-runs it at any time, and so does `acc doctor` in a terminal.
