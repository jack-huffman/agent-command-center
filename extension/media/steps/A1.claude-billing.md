# Agents bill your Claude subscription

`A1.claude-billing`

On your Claude subscription, agents' work counts toward the plan you already pay for; with an API key, or through Bedrock or Vertex, every token they use is billed on top, and nothing else tells you.

## How to fix it

Remove each setting the check names: take the variable out of your shell profile (such as ~/.zshrc) and open a new terminal, or delete apiKeyHelper, or the variable under "env", from the settings file it names. If Claude Code then asks you to sign in, use /login with your Claude account. Keeping one on purpose, such as a key you mean to bill per token, is fine: mark this done.

---

The step ticks itself when its check passes. **Check** re-runs it at any time, and so does `acc doctor` in a terminal.
